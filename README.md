### 摘要 / Abstract

#### 1. 研究对象与统一数学承载 / Research Subject and Unified Mathematical Substrate

本文围绕对象、规律、表示、允许动作与方法共同演化时的任务保持问题，将 G 框架的统一数学结构、计算范式与异构演化治理组织为一个连续体系。共同承载采用泛逻辑与泛迭代、高氏法空间及 GZ-GMS 富结构，以三层联络和主纤维丛广义非交换李代数描述非交换作用、跨层运输与高阶相容。状态、观察、运算、链、余链及证书均保留各自的类型、定义域和版本，使基础结构、求解方法与治理更新能够通过明确接口接续。

This article addresses task preservation when objects, laws, representations, admissible actions, and methods evolve jointly, bringing the unified mathematical structures, computational paradigms, and heterogeneous evolutionary governance of the G-Framework into a continuous system. Its common mathematical substrate comprises pan-logic analysis and pan-iterative analysis, GaoZheng Law-Space, and the rich structure of GZ-GMS. A triple-layer connection and the principal-bundle-based generalized noncommutative Lie algebra describe noncommutative actions, cross-layer transport, and higher-order compatibility. States, observations, operations, chains, cochains, and certificates retain their respective types, domains, and versions, allowing foundational structures, solution methods, and governance updates to connect through explicit interfaces.

#### 2. 计算范式与率—商统一 / Computational Paradigms and Rate–Quotient Unification

文章以性质商、同伦修复塔与总同调连接局部缺陷和整体接续，以 LHTS、TPH 及 PDE 的测度表达连接候选生成、路径搜索、残差验收与解释回读。任务充分商保存规定任务所需的区分，积分映射、棱柱同伦与微分交换则连接传统微积分和语义微积分，使变化计算、表示组织与合法求解迁移进入同一证书流程。“率”承担变化与积累的计算，“商”组织任务所需的区分，二者通过任务保持、结构相容与可核验见证共同下降。

The article connects local defects with global continuation through property quotients, homotopy repair towers, and total homology, and links candidate generation, path search, residual acceptance checks, and interpretive readback through LHTS, TPH, and measure-based PDE representations. Task-sufficient quotients preserve the distinctions required by the specified task, while integration maps, prism homotopies, and commutation with differentials connect traditional calculus with semantic calculus, bringing the computation of change, the organization of representations, and the admissible transfer of solution methods into a common certificate workflow. The “rate” component computes change and accumulation, while the “quotient” component organizes the distinctions required by the task; the two descend jointly through task preservation, structural compatibility, and verifiable witnesses.

#### 3. 语义微分与几何运输 / Semantic Differentials and Geometric Transport

语义微分从单个合法动作的前件、后继及观察差出发，经有限作用图的边界与对偶余链形成可逐项复算的逻辑推演和执行记录。几何部分以负曲率条件下的变分增长、有限观测差及其误差界为入口，通过纤维丛联络、平行运输、异构丛映射和积分接口，将几何数据接入任务观察与余链复形。几何承载、任务执行链和修复变更复形通过实际映射或桥接方程对接，并分别保留自身的数学类型与成立条件。

Semantic differentials start from the preconditions, successor, and observation difference associated with a single admissible action. Boundary maps of finite action graphs and dual cochains turn these data into logical inferences and execution records that can be recomputed item by item. The geometric component starts from variational growth under negative curvature, finite observation differences, and their error bounds. Bundle connections, parallel transport, heterogeneous bundle maps, and integration interfaces then connect geometric data to task observations and cochain complexes. The geometric substrate, task execution chains, and complexes of repair changes are connected through actual maps or bridging equations, each retaining its own mathematical type and conditions of validity.

#### 4. 同调修复与严格能力扩张 / Homological Repair and Strict Capability Expansion

代数部分以 Hom 复形中的闭缺陷、同伦填补、逐阶相容及有限矩阵不可解证书，刻画当前允许操作的修复边界；通过兼容扩张、最少新增方向和可执行前缀，把形式填补接续为实际合法修复。在一个完整参数族中，双曲几何输入、非零同调障碍、高阶运算与实际单位动作共享同一登记状态，线性残差与高阶相容缺陷在同一合法有限路径上共同归零。该构造同时给出旧动作族不可达、新动作族可达、最少新增方向和单位动作数下界，使相同任务与必要约束下的严格可达能力扩张具有明确见证。

The algebraic component characterizes the limits of repair under the currently admissible operations through closed defects in Hom complexes, homotopy fillings, order-by-order compatibility, and certificates that finite matrix systems have no solution. Compatible extensions, a minimum number of additional directions, and executable prefixes connect formal fillings to actual admissible repairs. In a complete parameterized family, hyperbolic geometric inputs, nonzero homological obstructions, higher-order operations, and actual unit actions share the same registered state, and both linear residuals and higher-order compatibility defects are reduced to zero along the same admissible finite path. The construction provides witnesses of unreachability under the original action family, reachability under the new family, the minimum number of additional directions, and lower bounds on the number of unit actions, thereby witnessing a strict expansion of reachable capabilities for the same task under the requisite constraints.

#### 5. 有限治理、能力复用与结构识别 / Finite Governance, Capability Reuse, and Structural Identification

任务正规型与有限状态细化提供精确回读、未来观察等价及策略复用，已验证结果连同其前提、适用域和版本进入可继续调用的能力库。法对象迁移通过状态与链的交换、逐原子判断运输、版本冻结和前提并集，保持相应有限治理更新与实际执行中的任务结论。结构类比、证明复用及已认证操作的有限生成闭包进一步把这一机制提升到方法层；有限显式任务模型中的规范编码与结构识别，则为共同任务核心的比较提供可终止的核验程序。

Task normal forms and finite-state refinements provide exact readback, equivalence of future observations, and strategy reuse. Verified results enter a reusable capability library together with their premises, domains of applicability, and versions. Migration of law objects preserves task conclusions during the corresponding finite governance updates and actual execution through commutation at the levels of states and chains, transport of individual atomic judgments, version freezing, and unions of premises. Structural analogy, proof reuse, and the finitely generated closure of certified operations extend this mechanism to the level of methods. Canonical encodings and structural identification in finite explicit task models provide terminating verification procedures for comparing shared task cores.

#### 6. 哲学收束与成立边界 / Philosophical Synthesis and Conditions of Validity

综合结果将基础结构、计算方法、跨域复用与异构治理收束为同一条方法主线：在明确任务和前提下保持观察、合法作用、结构相容与结果回读，以证书、来源和完整成本说明知识如何生成、迁移、识别与累积。数学有效性由表示充分、路径合法、结果可回读及成本可核算共同支撑。结构等价、求解迁移、有限终止和能力增量分别依赖相应的映射、误差、覆盖或构造见证；结构识别与实际承继的来源证据分别承担相应核验职责。具体应用中的几何响应、测量、算子、资源、实际性能与经验有效性，继续由对应输入、执行和外部验证实例化。

The combined results bring foundational structures, computational methods, cross-domain reuse, and heterogeneous governance into a common methodological approach: preserving observations, admissible actions, structural compatibility, and result readback under explicit tasks and premises, while using certificates, provenance, and full cost accounting to explain how knowledge is generated, transferred, identified, and accumulated. Mathematical validity is jointly supported by sufficient representations, admissible paths, results that can be read back, and costs that can be accounted for. Structural equivalence, solution transfer, termination in finitely many steps, and capability increments each depend on the corresponding maps, error bounds, coverage conditions, or constructive witnesses. Structural identification and provenance evidence for actual inheritance serve their respective verification purposes. In concrete applications, geometric responses, measurements, operators, resources, actual performance, and empirical validity continue to be instantiated through the corresponding inputs, execution, and external validation.

## 仓库目的

本仓库围绕后 AI 条件下的市场参与、价值形成与收入分配开展形式化经济研究，整理研究文稿及其配套参考资料。核心问题是：当部分产品的生产能力约束松弛、传统生产—就业—劳动收入—消费的传导减弱时，如何通过有效参与、贡献激励与底线保障，维持有真实资金来源、可持续且可核验的市场循环。

研究重点是“参与式分配、能者多得与坐标最小救济”的协同机制，并考察注意力与兴趣消费、组织合同、收益权与实际支付、治理公正和动态稳定之间的条件关系。文稿按明确的适用域、假设、证明和经验核验要求陈述结论。

上述 MIT 许可仅适用于对应脚本，不扩展到研究文稿或 Zenodo 来源资料。[docs/tex/LICENSE](docs/tex/LICENSE) 继续独立适用于 `docs/tex/` 中的文稿材料。其他文档按各自来源及许可声明处理。
